Digital transformation (modernisation planning)

  • Systems audit
  • Costed roadmap

Custom software

Software built around the way you already work, such as customer portals, internal tools and the links that let your systems share information. Built in stages you can see and test, then supported after launch.

Business management system (ERP)

One central system for sales, purchasing, stock, staff and finances, replacing the patchwork of spreadsheets and separate programs. Built around your processes, not a generic package.

Reporting dashboards (business intelligence)

See how your business is performing at a glance. Live, easy-to-read screens bring together figures from your accounts, tills and bookings, so decisions rest on facts, not guesswork.

Customer management (CRM and HubSpot)

A single, organised record of every customer and enquiry, so nothing slips through the cracks. We design it, set up HubSpot, connect it to your website, bookings and accounts, and train your team to use it with confidence.

Accounts automation

Sales, payments, fees, bills and invoices flow into Xero automatically and match up with your bank, with no re-typing and no month-end scramble. Works with Revolut Business, Stripe and your tills.

Payroll automation

Rosters, timesheets and clock-in times flow straight into payroll, with hours, tips and holiday pay calculated and recorded in your accounts for you. Fewer errors and less admin.

Stock and inventory control

Know what you have, where it is and when to reorder. Stock levels stay up to date across your tills, online shop and suppliers, with low-stock alerts and automatic reorder requests.

Tills and payments (POS)

Modern tills and card payment systems, set up or custom built, connected to your stock, online shop and accounts, so every sale is recorded once and reported correctly.

Online booking

Let customers book rooms, tables, classes, treatments or rentals online at any time of day, with secure payment, automatic reminders and live availability that stays in step with your hotel or booking software.

Event ticketing

Sell tickets online under your own brand, scan them at the door with a phone, control capacity, and have the money paid straight into your account.

Workflow automation

We remove repetitive manual work by getting your systems to pass information to each other on their own: new enquiries, orders, guest messages, reports and approvals. Fully documented, and yours to keep.

Microsoft 365 setup and management

Email, Teams, Word, Excel and cloud storage set up properly, with licences managed, accounts protected by two-step sign-in, data backed up, and a team on hand to manage users as your staff change.

Equipment and installation

Computers, networks, tills and card machines, plus Salto electronic door access and HikCentral security cameras, installed, set up and maintained by us so everything works together.

Facebook and Instagram advertising

Targeted campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), planned, built and measured against real bookings and sales, so you can see exactly what your advertising spend delivers.

Graphic design

Social media posts, adverts, menus, signage and brand materials designed in Canva, in formats your own team can easily edit and reuse.

Questions

Common questions, plainly answered

We have used the same systems for years. Do we have to replace everything?

Not necessarily. In most cases we connect and modernise what you already have first, and replace a system only when it genuinely holds you back. The systems audit shows you which is which.

I am not technical. Will I understand what you are telling me?

Yes. We explain everything in plain English and never assume technical knowledge. You will always know what we are doing, why we are doing it and what it costs.

Will the changes disrupt our day-to-day operations?

We plan every change to keep disruption to a minimum. We work in stages, test each stage with you, and avoid your busiest periods wherever we can.

Will my team be able to use the new systems?

Yes. We design for ease of use and show your team how each new system works, so they feel confident from day one.

Where do we start if we do not know what we need?

Start with our €500 systems audit. It shows you what is working, what is at risk and where time and money are being lost. You then receive a prioritised plan with costs, and you decide what to do first.

How much does a project cost?

Every engagement starts with the €500 systems audit. After that, we send a fixed quote for any work, so you know the cost before anything starts.

Is our data safe?

Security and backup are part of every project. We design systems so that card details are handled by regulated payment providers such as Stripe and Revolut, not stored on your own systems.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly support plans covering software updates, monitoring of your automations, and maintenance of equipment such as door access and security cameras.

Do you only work with hotels and restaurants?

No. We work with retail, online shops, clubs, venues, clinics and professional services. Hospitality is a large part of our work because it involves so many connected systems.

Do you manage Facebook and Instagram advertising every month?

We offer both one-off campaign builds and monthly management, including the designs and reporting tied to real sales.
