What we do

Everything your business runs on, built and looked after by one team.

Full stack software development

Web apps, customer portals, internal tools and APIs, from the database up to the interface. We scope it properly, ship in stages and support it after launch.

  • Web apps
  • APIs & integrations
  • Dashboards
  • Hosting & support

Digital transformation consultancy

A comprehensive audit of your systems, processes and costs, then a practical roadmap for what to automate, replace or connect first, and help delivering it.

Custom ERP development

One system for sales, purchasing, stock, staff and finance, built around how your business actually works instead of forcing you into an off-the-shelf package.

BI dashboard development

Live dashboards that pull from Xero, your POS, PMS and booking systems, so you see revenue, occupancy, margins and trends in one place.

HubSpot & CRM solutions

CRM design, HubSpot setup and integration with your website, booking and accounts systems, plus training so your team actually uses it.

Accounts automation

Sales, payouts, fees, bills and invoices flow into Xero on their own. Revolut Business, Stripe and your POS reconcile without the spreadsheets.

Payroll automation

Rosters, timesheets and clock-ins feed straight into payroll. Hours, tips and holiday pay are calculated and posted to your accounts automatically.

Integrated inventory management

Stock levels that stay in sync across your tills, Shopify or WooCommerce store and suppliers, with low-stock alerts and automatic purchase orders.

POS systems

Point-of-sale setup and custom POS builds that tie your tills, stock, online store and card terminals into one set of numbers.

Custom booking platforms

Booking engines for rooms, tables, classes, treatments and rentals, with payments, reminders and live availability that syncs to your PMS or channel manager.

Custom ticketing solutions

Event and venue ticketing on your own brand. Online sales, QR scanning at the door, capacity control, and payouts straight to your account.

Custom n8n workflows

We automate the repetitive work: lead capture, order routing, guest messaging, reporting and approvals. Self-hosted or cloud, documented, and yours to keep.

Microsoft 365 setup & management

Office 365 setup, licensing, email and Teams migration, security with MFA and device policies, backup, and ongoing user management.

Hardware & software installation

Business hardware and software installed, configured and maintained, including Salto access control and HikCentral CCTV, networks, terminals and workstations.

Meta ads marketing

Facebook and Instagram campaigns planned, built and optimised against real bookings and sales, with the pixel and Conversions API set up properly.

Canva graphic design

Social posts, ad creative, menus, signage and brand kits built in Canva so your team can edit and reuse them.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with businesses outside hospitality?

Yes. We work with retail, e-commerce, clubs, venues, clinics and professional services. Hospitality is a big part of our work because it touches so many systems at once.

Can you connect our existing systems instead of replacing them?

Usually, yes. Most of our projects connect tools you already pay for, like Xero, Stripe, Mews, SiteMinder and Shopify, using their APIs and n8n.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly support plans covering software updates, workflow monitoring, and hardware maintenance for systems such as Salto and HikCentral.

Where do we start if we don't know what we need?

Start with a conversation. Every client onboarding begins with a comprehensive audit of your systems, so we can show you what's working, what's at risk and where time and money are being lost. You then get a prioritised plan with costs and decide what to do first.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote so you know the cost before any work starts.

Do you run Meta ads on a retainer?

We offer both one-off campaign builds and monthly management, including creative made in Canva and reporting tied to real sales.
