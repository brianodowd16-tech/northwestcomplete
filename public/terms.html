Terms & Conditions

Draft for the club committee to review. Confirm against your lottery licence/permit before going live.

  1. Promoter. The Christmas Holiday & Fuel Draw is run by St Michael's GAA & LGFA (the "Club"). All proceeds go to club development.
  2. Eligibility. Entrants must be aged 18 or over. Club committee members involved in running the draw may not win.
  3. Tickets. Each ticket gets a unique number, shown on screen when payment is confirmed. Bundle prices are listed on the ticket page. Tickets are non-refundable unless the draw is cancelled.
  4. Closing date. Online sales close at the time shown on the website. Tickets bought after that will be refunded.
  5. The draw. Winning numbers will be drawn at random on the date shown, in the presence of club officers, and streamed on the Club's Facebook page.
  6. Prizes. Prizes are as announced by the Club before the draw. Prizes are provided by the sponsors and venues named and are subject to their terms, including booking, travel-date and delivery conditions. There is no cash alternative unless the Club says otherwise.
  7. Winners. Winners will be contacted using the email and phone number given at checkout. Winners' first names and area may be published.
  8. Payments & data. Payments are processed by Stripe. The Club stores your name, email, phone and ticket numbers only to run the draw and contact winners.
  9. Licence. Licence/permit details to be confirmed.
