# Malta-first playbook

Offer: **Comprehensive systems audit, €500 fixed price**. Landing page: `northwestcomplete.com/malta.html` (use `?utm_source=...` links so every booking shows its source).

## Who to win first
1. **Hospitality and tourism:** hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, dive and activity operators, spas. Lead with Mews, SiteMinder, Try.be, Salto, online booking and payments that reach Xero on their own. Proof: Drumhierny Woodland Hideaway.
2. **Property and professional services:** agencies, accountants, lawyers, consultancies. Lead with CRM (Odoo or Salesforce), Microsoft 365 security and accounts automation.
3. **Retail and online shops:** Shopify, WooCommerce, tills, stock.

## Where the owners are (approach all of them, in this order)
- **Introducers who see the mess first:** accountants, bookkeepers and Xero advisers. Offer each a simple recommendation arrangement.
- **Trade bodies and events:** Malta Hotels and Restaurants Association, Malta Chamber of Commerce, GRTU Malta Chamber of SMEs, Malta Business Bureau, Malta Tourism Authority events. Offer a 20-minute "stop paying twice for software" talk or webinar.
- **LinkedIn, by hand:** 20 personal connection requests a day to owners and general managers in the segments above (no automation tools, they break LinkedIn's rules). Follow with a short message, then a call.
- **Meta ads:** Malta-targeted, owners and managers, to `malta.html`. Retarget everyone who visits.
- **Direct:** phone and email the front-of-house and ops leaders at 10 target businesses a day.
