Terms and refund policy

Plain-English terms for the comprehensive systems audit, including how cancellations and refunds work.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

These terms apply to purchases from northwestcomplete.com. We are Northwest Complete Limited ("Northwest Complete", "we", "us"). You can reach us at hello@northwestcomplete.com.

The comprehensive systems audit

The audit is a fixed-price review of how your business's technology works today. It covers your software and subscriptions, how your systems share information, equipment and network, security and backup, and technology costs. You receive our findings explained in plain English, together with a prioritised action plan and costs.

The price is €500. Northwest Complete is not currently VAT registered, so no VAT is added. The audit is a stand-alone service: it does not commit you to buying any further work from us.

Paying

You pay by card on the secure payment page of our payment provider, myPOS. We never see or store your card details. Your booking is confirmed once myPOS confirms your payment, and we then email you a confirmation and contact you to arrange the audit.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel at any time before we begin work on your audit for a full refund. Email us and we will refund the full €500 to the card you paid with.
  • If we are unable to carry out the audit for any reason, we will refund you in full.
  • Once we have begun the audit, the fee is not refundable, but if you are unhappy with what we deliver, tell us and we will put it right.
  • Refunds are normally returned to your card within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

Your information

To do the audit we may need to see how your systems are set up. We treat everything you share as confidential, use it only to carry out your audit, and ask only for the access we need. See our privacy notice for how we handle personal data.

Liability

We carry out every audit with reasonable skill and care. Our findings are professional recommendations, and you decide whether to act on them. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under law that cannot be limited.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland, and the Irish courts have jurisdiction over any dispute, unless the law where you live gives you the right to bring a claim elsewhere.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email hello@northwestcomplete.com. We will explain it in plain English.
