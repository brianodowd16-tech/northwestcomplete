Privacy notice

What we collect, why we collect it, and your rights, in plain English.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

We collect only what we need to reply to you, take your booking and carry out your work. We never see your card details, we do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website.

Who is responsible

Northwest Complete Limited is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at hello@northwestcomplete.com.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiries: your name, email, business, and message, so we can reply and discuss your needs.
  • Audit bookings: your name, email, phone, business, any notes you add, and confirmation of payment from myPOS, so we can arrange the audit, issue your invoice and keep our accounts.
  • Where you found us: if you arrive from one of our social media links, we note which one with your enquiry, so we know which of our marketing works. It is kept only for the duration of your visit and sent with your enquiry.

Our legal bases are taking steps at your request before and during a contract, our legal obligations (such as keeping accounts), and our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run the business, and they handle your data only on our instructions: myPOS (card payments), Xero (invoicing and accounts), Odoo (customer records), and Hostinger (website hosting and email). Some of these providers are outside the European Economic Area and rely on approved safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years unless they lead to work. We keep invoices and payment records for 6 years, as required for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Email us and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses temporary browser storage only to remember which campaign link brought you here, and it is cleared when you close the tab.
